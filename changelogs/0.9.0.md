# Guild Found Market 0.9.0

A feature release: search any item straight from your bags, edit your listings without a bank trip, and a new Options panel to turn features on or off. Grab it from CurseForge and run a full game restart (not just `/reload`), since the addon version changed.

## Added

**Alt-click an item to search it.**
Alt + left-click any item in your bags or bank to open Guild Found Market and search for that item right away. It works the same with the default Blizzard bags and with popular bag addons (Baganator, Bagnon, Combuctor, AdiBags, BetterBags). It is off by default; turn it on in the new Options tab. Some bag addons use Alt-click for their own features, so disable it again if it clashes.

**Options tab.**
A gear button next to Help opens a new Options panel where you can turn features on or off, each with a short description on mouseover. It currently covers the minimap button, alt-click search, and importing item names from the aux addon. Your choices are saved per account.

## Changed

**Edit your listings in place.**
Every item on the My Items tab now has an Edit button. It loads the item into the panel at the bottom so you can change the quantity or price and click Update. Editing does not require the item to be in your bags, so you no longer have to travel to a bank and relist just to adjust a number.

**Ctrl-click your own listings to search.**
Ctrl-click an item on the My Items tab to jump to the Buy tab and find who else is selling it, matching the Ctrl-click you already had in the Sellers and Browse lists.

## Note

Settings are stored per account. If you previously hid the minimap button or disabled the aux seed, those choices carry over into the new Options panel automatically.
