# Guild Found Market 0.19.1

The Buyers tab opens on everything the confederation wants to buy, the find-buyers coin shows its
side window in place, the category browser is complete again, and the browse cap is a slider.

## Added

**The Buyers tab opens on everything wanted.**
Entering the tab now collects every buyer's full want list up front: one buyer-scan broadcast,
then a directed fetch per answering buyer (the same shape as the bag scan), and all wants appear
as Item | Qty | Price | Buyer rows, item-sortable like the other lists. Click a row for that
buyer's want list filtered to the item, right-click to whisper them, ctrl-click to find sellers.
The search box does two jobs: typing filters the collected wants live, and picking an item from
the autocomplete still asks the network directly for that item, refreshing those rows in place.
Refresh re-collects everything. The status line warns when the buyer scan hit its size cap (the
Sellers & Buyers scan size option).

**Find buyers opens the buyers window in place.**
The coin button on My Items > WTS rows no longer jumps to the Buyers tab: it fires the same
who-wants-this-item query but shows the replies in the buyers side window, without leaving My
Items. Same rules as on the Scan tab: best payer on top, right-click a name to whisper, and a
Send button per buyer that pre-fills a COD mail with the wanted amount clamped to what your bags
hold. The window closes when you navigate away from the WTS view, mirroring how it closes with
the Scan tab.

**The category browser is complete again.**
Classes with a single generic subclass (Consumable, Reagent) had dropped out of the sidebar; they
are back as class-level entries that browse the whole category in one click. An Armor subclass is
now browsable whole too: selecting Cloth queries every cloth slot at once, like the Auction House,
while still expanding the slot list underneath for narrowing down to Head or Chest. Consumable
sits below Recipe in the list.

**Browse results shown is now a slider.**
The category browser showed at most 150 results and asked you to narrow down beyond that. The cap
is now configurable in Options under Network (50 to 600, default 150), and moving the slider
re-cuts an open result list right away. Purely client-side, like the scan size.
