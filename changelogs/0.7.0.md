# Guild Found Market 0.7.0

A big quality release: your listings now show **exactly** what is for sale (random enchants included), soulbound junk can no longer clutter the market, and a nasty bug that quietly ate people's listings is gone for good.

Grab it from CurseForge and run a full game restart (not just `/reload`), since the addon version changed.

## New

**Random-enchant items finally show what you are actually buying.**
Before, a "... of the Bear" or "... of the Eagle" item just showed up as a vague "random enchantment" with no stats, so buyers had no idea what they were getting. Now every listing captures the specific variant: the Buy results, a seller's catalog, and your own My Items all show the real name and the exact stats when you hover. You can also list several variants of the same base item side by side, and when you search, each seller's result is tagged with its suffix (for example "of the Eagle") so you can tell them apart at a glance. Plain items without a random enchant are unchanged.

**Soulbound items can no longer be listed.**
If every copy of an item in your bags is bound to you (soulbound, or a quest item), you simply cannot offer it, because it could never be traded anyway. No more dead listings for gear you are wearing or quest items you picked up by accident. Stock you keep on a bank alt is unaffected, since the addon does not police what it cannot see.

**See your own offer while you search.**
Search for an item you also sell, and your own offer now shows up right in the results, in the cheapest-first order and marked "(you)". You can instantly see where your price sits against the other sellers and adjust to stay competitive. It even shows while your listings are paused, since it is purely your own view.

**Sortable Sellers list.**
On the Sellers tab you can now click the Seller or Items column header to sort, and click again to flip the direction. The active column shows a little arrow. It defaults to most items first, and the name filter keeps working alongside whatever sort you pick.

## Fixed

**Listings no longer vanish after a loading screen.**
This was the big one. Several people lost their whole shop after hopping a zeppelin or zoning into a city. The cause: the addon double-checked your offers against your bags, and during a loading screen (or even while splitting a stack) your inventory reads as momentarily empty, which permanently deleted the listing or shrank its quantity. That check is gone. A listing is now treated as your claim, never auto-edited and never auto-removed; whether you actually have the goods is settled the normal way, in the whisper between buyer and seller. As a nice side effect, you can stash stock on a bank alt and advertise it from your main.

**Shop links work for everyone now, and search stays reliable.**
Clicking a shop link did nothing for anyone also running NovaWorldBuffs or Questie (their code threw a harmless looking error that quietly killed our click handler). On top of that, an earlier version of the link handler could "taint" the chat frames and intermittently block the addon's own channel messages, which broke search and the seller scan at random. Both are fixed with a clean, taint-free approach: links open reliably with no error, and search and the scan are rock solid.

**Switching tabs no longer throws an error** caused by leftover rows from the previous tab being reused for a split second.

## Updating from an older version

Your existing listings are kept automatically, but two kinds are worth a quick tidy:

- **Random-enchant gear you already listed** was stored without its suffix, so it now shows as the plain base item with no stats. Re-add those items (pick them up and Offer again) to get the proper "of the ..." name and stats, then remove the old plain entry with the X.
- **Soulbound items you already listed** stay in your list and are not cleaned up automatically (your list is yours). The new block only stops you adding bound items going forward, so remove any old soulbound listings yourself with the X.

## Notes

This release changes the messages the addon sends, so for cross-guild search to keep working, everyone in your confederation should update to 0.7.0. Solo testing works on its own.

Thanks for the bug reports and ideas, keep them coming.
