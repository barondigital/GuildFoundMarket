# Guild Found Market 0.20.0

GFM now shows whether a seller or buyer is verified by the SoD Guild Found addon (no tampering
recorded), everywhere their name appears, and the COD Send buttons refuse recipients who are not.

## Added

**Guild Found verification on every player.**
If you run the SoD Guild Found addon, your GFM now attaches its verdict about your own character
("no tampering recorded", the same status it enforces when you trade) to the replies GFM already
sends: search results, the Sellers and Buyers indexes, buyer-find replies and category browse
answers. Everyone receiving them remembers the status per name. Hover any player, anywhere, and
the tooltip says where they stand: |cff66ff66verified, no tampering recorded|r,
|cffff7300NOT verified, tampering recorded|r, or |cffffd100unverified|r when no status was
received. That last one is also what an older GFM client, or a player without the Guild Found
addon, shows up as: no proof means unverified, never a pass.

**Rows are tinted to match.**
A row belonging to a player with tampering recorded gets an orange background tint; one whose
status is unknown gets a soft yellow tint. Verified players' rows stay clean. This works in the
Buy results, the Sellers and Buyers tabs (indexes and open catalogs), the category browser, the
COD list and the buyers side window.

**COD Send refuses unverified recipients.**
With the check on, the Send button next to a COD order (COD tab) and per buyer in the buyers side
window greys out when the recipient is not verified, and hovering it explains why. The mailbox
send-assist itself refuses too, so every path to a COD mail is covered. Trades and mail you do by
hand stay the Guild Found addon's own business; GFM only guards what GFM prepares.

**New option: Check players' Guild Found status.**
On by default, under Options > Guild Found. It needs the SoD Guild Found addon on YOUR client
too: without it the checkbox is locked off and its tooltip says so. The check is local only;
turning it off hides the tints and unblocks the buttons for you, but your own status is still
sent to others whenever the Guild Found addon is present, so you always keep proving yourself.

## Compatibility

The status travels as one extra field appended to the end of the existing messages, exactly like
the guild tag before it. Older GFM clients ignore it and keep working unchanged; their replies
simply show here as unverified until they update.
