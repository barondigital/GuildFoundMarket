# Guild Found Market 0.22.0

Two features for buyers and crafters: BYOM tags on craft-on-request listings, and Browse
that opens straight on the category of the item you searched.

## Added

**BYOM: mark a craftable as "bring your own materials".**
Crafters kept getting whispers from buyers expecting ready stock plus mats. A seller who
crafts on request can now flag such a listing as BYOM. Buyers see an orange `[BYOM]` tag on
the listing in search results, category browse and shop catalogs, and the item tooltip
explains it: the seller crafts this on request, you supply the mats.

Set the flag with the checkbox in the compose panel on My Items (greyed out on items you
can't craft, with a tooltip explaining why), or flip it per listing in the new BYOM column
next to Bag sync. The flag is only offered on items your character can actually craft: GFM
learns your recipes whenever a profession window is open, so open each of your professions
once. On the wire the flag rides append-only (a 5th row column on catalogs and browse
replies, a 9th search-reply field): older clients interoperate unchanged and unflagged rows
stay byte-identical.

**Browse opens on your searched item's category.**
Searching an item on Buy and clicking Browse now lands directly on that item's own class and
subclass (and armor slot when it has one), with the sidebar expanded and the category query
already fired, instead of the last category you browsed. The jump happens once per search,
so a category you pick by hand afterwards sticks on later toggles.

The new option "Browse filters on your searched item" (default on) also pre-fills the name
filter with the searched item, so you start from exactly that item across all sellers. Turn
it off to land on the full category unfiltered.
