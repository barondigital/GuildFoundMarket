# Guild Found Market 0.18.1

The bag scan gets a proper home.

## Changed

**The bag scan has its own Scan tab.**
Introduced in 0.18.0 as a pop-over window on My Items, the scan now lives on a tab of its own, next
to My Items. The Scan bags and Stop buttons and the results list moved there, with more rows and
wider columns, and the My Items tab keeps its space for composing offers. A running scan carries on
while you visit other tabs (or close the window); the Scan tab shows the current state the moment
you return.
