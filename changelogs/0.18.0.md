# Guild Found Market 0.18.0

Price-check your whole inventory against the market in one click, and see exactly how busy the
marketplace traffic is.

## Added

**Scan bags: market prices for everything you carry.**
A new "Scan bags" button beside Offer on My Items finds every sellable item in your bags (not
soulbound, no quest items; your bank is included while its window is open) and checks what the
confederation currently asks for each. Under the hood it sends one "who's selling" broadcast and
then fetches each online seller's catalog with the same directed whispers the Sellers tab uses, so
for every seller the load equals one buyer opening their shop once. A window shows live progress
("fetching price catalogs 37/153") with a Stop button; stopping keeps the prices found so far, and
closing the window lets the scan finish in the background. Each row shows your stock, your current
listing and the market low/high with seller count; hover the market column for the individual
sellers and their prices. Every scan also snapshots the whole sweep into the local price database,
so item tooltips show fresh ranges afterwards.

**Network health view.**
A Network button next to Debug on the Help tab opens a plain-language view of this session's
marketplace traffic: whispers sent and received (with per-minute rates), channel broadcasts seen,
server slow-downs, dropped outgoing replies, send-queue depth, and replies that arrived after a
scan's collection window. Every number comes with a one-line explanation, and a green/yellow/red
verdict at the top sums up the worst signal. Open to everyone, so guildmates can compare numbers
when tuning their settings.

## Changed

**The scan size cap is now yours to set.**
How many sellers/buyers one scan collects (previously fixed at 150) is now a slider in Options
under Network, from 50 to 600. It's purely client-side: it only limits what your own client keeps,
everyone answers regardless. The Network view shows whether your scans actually hit it.
