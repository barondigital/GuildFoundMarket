# Guild Found Market 0.8.0

Browse the market by category, see at a glance which version you run, and a cross-version fix so prices and locations stop looking garbled while people are updating.

Grab it from CurseForge and run a full game restart (not just `/reload`), since the addon version changed.

## New

**Browse by category.**
Until now you could only search one item by name. The Buy tab now has a **Browse** toggle, with a **Category to Subclass** sidebar on the left (in the same order as the Auction House: Weapons, Armor, and so on). Pick a subcategory like Weapons then Daggers and you see every offer in it at once, across all online sellers.

The results show the item (coloured by quality), its required level, quantity, price and the seller. You can sort by quality (click the Item header), required level or price, and narrow a busy category with a level range or a text filter. Click a seller's name to jump straight into their full list. Single-bucket categories without real subcategories (Consumables, Reagents, Junk) are left out, since browsing those is just one giant list anyway; you can still find them with Search.

**Version display and update notice.**
Your installed version now shows in the top-right corner of the window. The addon also notices when someone in your confederation is running a newer version, and tells you to update, so you are never silently out of date.

## Fixed

**Prices and locations no longer garble across versions.**
0.7.0 introduced random-enchant variants by inserting an extra field in the middle of the search and catalog replies. For anyone still on 0.6.0 that shifted everything by one column, so prices showed up as "1c" and locations showed a number. The extra field is now added at the **end** of the message instead, so old and new clients read each other correctly again (old clients simply do not show the variant). The rule from now on: optional fields always go at the end, never in the middle, so an update never breaks older clients.

## Important: if you are on 0.7.0, please update

0.7.0's message format is **not** compatible with 0.6.0 or 0.8.0. If you grabbed 0.7.0 in the short window it was up, update to **0.8.0**; that is what fixes the garbled prices and locations. 0.6.0 and 0.8.0 work together fine (0.6.0 users just will not see random-enchant variants), so the whole confederation does not have to update in lockstep, but everyone should move off 0.7.0.

## Notes

The category browse adds new messages over the marketplace channel, so only updated clients answer category queries; everyone should be on 0.8.0+ to get the most out of it. Item search, the Sellers tab and My Items keep working across 0.6.0 and 0.8.0.

Thanks for testing and for the reports.
