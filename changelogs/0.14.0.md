# Guild Found Market 0.14.0

Recent prices, right on the item tooltip. GFM now remembers what it last saw for an item and shows
it when you hover, so you can glance at the going rate from anywhere.

## Added

**Recent prices on item tooltips.**
GFM keeps a small local snapshot per item, built purely from data you already receive (item
searches, category browses, and seller views). On an item's tooltip it adds:

```
GFM: 3 sellers · 1.3.34 - 2.5.0
GFM: scanned 5m ago
```

- It's a snapshot, not a history: each new observation overwrites the last, so offline sellers'
  prices drop off and a re-scan refreshes it. The seller count and age tell you how complete and
  fresh it is.
- Prices show in a compact coloured gold/silver/copper notation (`1g3s34c` -> `1.3.34`, gold in
  gold, silver in white, copper in copper). Bids (no price) are excluded from the range.
- Items GFM hasn't seen yet show "GFM: alt click to scan".
- Toggle it all in Options with "Show recent prices on item tooltips" (on by default).

## Changed

**Alt-click to search is on by default.**
Alt + left-click an item in your bags or bank to search it on GFM (which also feeds the price
snapshot). If it clashes with a bag addon's own Alt-click, turn it off in Options.

## Not changing

No protocol change: the price data is built locally from search/browse/seller replies you already
receive, and is keyed by numeric itemID, so it's language-independent.
