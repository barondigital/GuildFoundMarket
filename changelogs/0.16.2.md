# Guild Found Market 0.16.2

The Announce button can now post your shop note instead of the default line.

## Added

**Announce your shop note.**
A new option (off by default) in the Options panel. When it's on and you have a shop note set,
pressing Announce composes your note followed by your clickable shop link, `<your note> @[Name's
shop]`, rather than the plain "Shop is open!" line. With the option off, or no note set, the
default announce line is unchanged. As always, nothing is sent for you: the line lands in your
chat box so you can add items or text and press Enter yourself.
