# Guild Found Market 0.18.4

List scanned items straight from the Scan tab, at an undercut, average or matching market price.

## Added

**List items straight from the Scan tab.**
Every scanned row that found a market price now has a checkbox, with a select-all box next to the
List column header. Tick the items you want (hovering a checkbox previews the exact price it would
use) and press List selected: each ticked item becomes a listing at a price derived from the scan.
Three price modes, switchable on the Scan tab itself and in Options under Selling:

- **Undercut**: 1 copper under the lowest market price found.
- **Average**: a robust market average. Prices below half or above double the median are dropped
  first, so one extreme listing can't drag the result.
- **Match**: exactly the lowest market price found.

An item you already list keeps its quantity and Bag sync switch; only its price is updated. A new
listing goes up for the full scanned count. Rows without a priced offer (bids only, or nothing
found) can't be ticked, since there is no market price to derive anything from.
