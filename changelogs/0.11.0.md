# Guild Found Market 0.11.0

Browse armor by equip slot: armor subclasses in the Browse sidebar now expand to a third level (Head, Shoulder, Chest, and so on), so you can search a single slot instead of the whole subclass. The query change is append only, so it stays compatible with 0.10.x and mixed guilds keep working.

## Added

**Browse armor by equip slot.**
In the Browse sidebar, armor subclasses (Cloth, Leather, Mail, Plate, and the accessories group) now open to a third level by equip slot: Head, Shoulder, Chest, Wrist, Hands, Waist, Legs, Feet, and Back under Cloth, plus Neck, Finger, Trinket, and Held In Off-hand for accessories. Picking a slot narrows the search to exactly that slot. Robes are grouped under Chest. Subclasses without a useful slot split, such as shields and relics, keep querying the whole subclass as before.

## Compatibility

The category query carries the chosen slot as a new trailing field, so 0.10.x clients ignore it and reply with the whole subclass; your client then filters to the slot. Buyers and sellers on 0.10.x and 0.11.0 interoperate without any changes.
