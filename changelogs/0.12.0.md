# Guild Found Market 0.12.0

Sortable, searchable item lists and a per-seller shop note. The note is delivered cheaply: the seller scan carries only a one-byte "has note" flag, and the text itself is fetched on demand in new messages that older clients ignore, so 0.11.x stays compatible.

## Added

**Sort the item lists.**
Click a column header to sort Buy search, Buy browse, the seller view and My Items. Qty, Price and Lvl (where the column is shown) toggle ascending and descending. The Item header cycles through four states on repeated clicks: quality descending, quality ascending, alphabetical ascending, alphabetical descending. The lists open alphabetically; Buy results keep sorting Seller as a plain name toggle.

**Find an item in a list.**
My Items and the open seller's view each get a "Find item:" box that filters the already-loaded items by name (substring) as you type.

**Full-row hover highlight.**
Hovering anywhere on a row now lights up the whole row instead of only the item-name cell.

**Shop note.**
Sellers can set a one-line note in My Items (up to 240 bytes) that buyers can read. In the Sellers list, a chat-bubble icon marks sellers who have a note; clicking it loads the note and a hover then shows it. Opening a seller shows the note in an outlined "Shop note" block at the bottom of their view, delivered together with their catalog so no click is needed there.

## Compatibility

The seller scan reply gains a one-byte trailing "has note" flag (append only, ignored by 0.11.x). The note text never rides the scan: it is fetched on demand through new `NQ`/`NR` messages, and bundled alongside the catalog reply when you open a seller. Older clients do not answer those messages, so they simply show no note; the existing search, scan and catalog formats are unchanged. Buyers and sellers on 0.11.x and 0.12.0 interoperate without any changes.
