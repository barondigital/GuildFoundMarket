# Guild Found Market 0.8.1

0.8.1 is 0.8.0 plus two quick fixes. If you are coming from 0.6.0 or 0.7.0, this is the big "browse the market" release, so here is everything that is new.

Grab it from CurseForge and run a full game restart (not just `/reload`), since the addon version changed.

## New

**Browse by category.**
Until now you could only search one item by name. The Buy tab now opens on a **Browse** view, with a **Category to Subclass** sidebar on the left (in the same order as the Auction House: Weapons, Armor, and so on). Pick a subcategory like Weapons then Daggers and you see every offer in it at once, across all online sellers. Use the toggle in the top right to switch back to item search.

The results show the item (coloured by quality), its required level, quantity, price and the seller. You can sort by quality (click the Item header), required level or price, and narrow a busy category with a level range or a text filter. Click a seller's name to jump straight into their full list, or ctrl-click an item to search who else sells it. Single-bucket categories without real subcategories (Consumables, Reagents, Junk) are left out, since browsing those is just one giant list anyway; you can still find them with Search.

**Version display and update notice.**
Your installed version now shows in the top-right corner of the window. The addon also notices when someone in your confederation is running a newer version, and tells you to update, so you are never silently out of date.

## Fixed

**Prices and locations no longer garble across versions.**
0.7.0 introduced random-enchant variants by inserting an extra field in the middle of the search and catalog replies. For anyone still on 0.6.0 that shifted everything by one column, so prices showed up as "1c" and locations showed a number. The extra field is now added at the **end** of the message instead, so old and new clients read each other correctly again (old clients simply do not show the variant).

**Ctrl-click an item always opens the search (0.8.1).**
Ctrl-clicking an item in a seller's list or in the category browse could leave you in the Browse view; it now jumps straight to the item search for that item.

**Browse is the default view (0.8.1).**
Opening the Buy tab now shows the category browse first, with the toggle to switch to item search.

## Important: if you are on 0.7.0, please update

0.7.0's message format is **not** compatible with 0.6.0 or 0.8.x. If you grabbed 0.7.0 in the short window it was up, update to **0.8.1**; that is what fixes the garbled prices, locations and the occasional wrong item name (a seller's price read as a fake "of the ..." affix). 0.6.0 and 0.8.x work together fine (0.6.0 users just will not see random-enchant variants), so the whole confederation does not have to update in lockstep, but everyone should move off 0.7.0.

## Notes

The category browse adds new messages over the marketplace channel, so only updated clients answer category queries; everyone should be on 0.8.x to get the most out of it. Item search, the Sellers tab and My Items keep working across 0.6.0 and 0.8.x.

Thanks for testing and for the reports.
