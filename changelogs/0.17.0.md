# Guild Found Market 0.17.0

Cash On Delivery orders: capture a buyer's COD request as a to-do item, work the list at a mailbox,
and let the send-assist pre-fill the mail for you.

## Added

**Cash On Delivery orders.**
A new COD tab on My Items keeps a per-character to-do list of the Cash On Delivery mails you owe
buyers. Keep your shop open while you're out and about, then work the list at a mailbox later. Each
row shows the item, quantity, the amount to collect, and the buyer. Right-click the item to whisper
the buyer; Edit reopens the order in the add form; Done clears it once it's mailed.

**Buyers request a COD from your listing.**
With "Accept COD order requests" turned on (Options, off by default), other GFM users can Alt-click
one of your rows on the Buy results, your shop catalogue, or the category Browse to ask for a COD.
The order is added to your list and the buyer gets an automatic confirmation whisper. The whisper is
configurable (Options), with the tokens `%item`, `%qty`, `%unit`, `%total`, and `%buyer`, and a
Reset to default button that restores the example with every placeholder in place. Your own listed
price is authoritative, and requests for items you don't currently list, or made while your listings
are offline, are declined automatically.

**Add a COD by hand.**
When a deal happens through chat, add the order yourself on the COD tab: buyer (autocompleted from
your guild roster), item (search or shift-click a link), quantity, and unit price.

**The request popup knows your outstanding order and your free stock.**
Alt-clicking opens a small quantity box at once, showing "checking with the seller" while it asks,
then filling in how many of that item you already have on order with them. Changing the number
updates that one order rather than stacking duplicates. On a listing that follows your bags, the
popup caps the quantity to what you actually have free (open COD orders reserve stock), snapping a
too-large entry back down, and an item fully spoken for by CODs drops out of search and your shop
until an order is mailed or cleared. My Items marks how much of a stock is reserved.

**Cancel a COD from chat.**
The confirmation whisper carries a clickable Cancel COD link, so a buyer can drop the whole order
even after the item has sold out of the listings and can no longer be clicked there. Setting the
quantity to 0 in the popup cancels too.

**Mailbox send-assist.**
Standing at a mailbox, the Send button on a COD row pre-fills the Send Mail frame for you: the
recipient, the item pulled from your bags (exactly the ordered quantity, splitting a larger stack
rather than over-sending), the COD amount to collect, and a subject. You review it and press Send
yourself, so real gold and items only ever move on your own click.
