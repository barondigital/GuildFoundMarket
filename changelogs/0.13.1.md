# Guild Found Market 0.13.1

A small follow-up to 0.13.0: the shop note now appears on the Buyers side too.

## Changed

**Note on the Buyers side.**
A player's note (set on the My Items tab) now shows wherever that player appears:
- the **Buyers index** gets a chat-bubble next to buyers who have a note (click to load it, then
  hover to read), exactly like the Sellers index;
- an opened buyer's want list shows the note in an outlined **Note** block at the bottom, bundled
  with their want-list reply.

The note is the same per-character note, fetched on demand, so older clients stay compatible.
