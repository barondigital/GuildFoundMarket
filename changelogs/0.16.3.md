# Guild Found Market 0.16.3

Open a shop straight to the item you were looking at, and reset any search or filter box in one click.

## Added

**Opening a shop from an item jumps straight to that item.**
Open a seller from a Buy result or a Browse row and their shop now opens pre-filtered to that item,
rather than their whole catalogue. The Buyers side mirrors it: opening a buyer from a "who wants
this?" hit filters their want list to the item. Clear the filter box to see everything again, and
opening a seller or buyer from the plain index still shows their full list. It filters on the item
name, so every variant of a random-enchant item shows too. This is a lighter alternative to
scrolling-to-and-highlighting a row, which was jittery while a catalogue is still streaming in.

**A one-click reset on every search and filter box.**
Every search, find, and filter field (item search, Find seller, Find buyer, the seller and buyer
catalogue filters, the My Items and My WTB filters, and the Browse filter) now shows a small clear
button as soon as it holds text. Clicking it empties the field and resets what it was showing,
exactly like pressing Escape while the box is focused.
