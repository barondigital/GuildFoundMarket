# Guild Found Market 0.17.4

Turn a buyer's whisper into a COD with one click, with clearer confirmations all round.

## Added

**One-click COD from a buyer's whisper.**
When a buyer whispers you an item link for one of your listings ("[Citrine]@1g still in stock?"), a
clickable `[Create COD]` is appended to their message in your chat. Click it and a small popup asks
the quantity (defaulting to 1, capped to your free stock on a bag-synced listing), then the COD order
is placed at your listed price, with the same on-screen flash and confirmation whisper as a requested
one. It only appears for items you currently list. Turn it off with "Add a Create COD link to buyer
whispers" in Options.

**A link to the full changelog history.**
The changelog overlay now has a line at the bottom linking to the complete changelog history on
GitHub. Addons can't open web pages, so it's a read-only box: click it, then Ctrl+C to copy the URL.

## Changed

**The quantity popup opens above the chat line and has a close button.**
It no longer covers your chat input, and you can dismiss it with the X (or Escape).

**Buyer confirmations show in chat.**
Requesting a COD, and the result of clicking a Cancel COD link (cancelled, or nothing to cancel), now
print to chat rather than only the window's status line, so you always see them even with the window
closed. The wording is clearer too, leading with "Your COD ..." so it's obvious the message is your
confirmation.
