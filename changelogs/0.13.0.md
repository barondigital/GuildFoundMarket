# Guild Found Market 0.13.0

The buy side. Alongside selling, you can now publish what you want to buy and find buyers for
the items you have. Stays compatible with 0.12.x: the new buyer queries are messages older
clients simply don't answer, and the existing search/scan/catalog formats are unchanged.

## Added

**WTB (Want To Buy).**
My Items splits into two sub-tabs: **Selling** (your listings, unchanged) and **WTB**, your list
of wanted items. Add a want through the search-autocomplete picker with a quantity, an optional
price, and a **COD** checkbox (when COD is on, a price is required: it's the amount paid on
delivery). No ownership check, since you're looking for something you don't have yet.

**Buyers tab.**
A new top-level tab next to Sellers, with two modes you toggle between:
- **Search by item** (the default): pick an item and see who wants it, with their quantity, price
  and COD. This is also where the coin "Find buyers" button on each Selling row takes you.
- **Find buyer**: browse all online buyers (name filter + sortable headers), and open one to see
  their full wanted list.
Whisper a buyer straight from the list. Every Buyers view has the same sorting, text filter and
full-row hover highlight as the Sellers side.

## Changed

**Price format is now a fill format.**
Every price field accepts both notations (`2g3s44c` and `3.50`) regardless of the setting. The
**Price fill format** option only chooses how a price is shown back to you (the edit prefill and
the example), not what you're allowed to type.

**Compose quality-of-life.**
Item pickers open downward with arrow-key navigation, and Tab moves between fields (Item → Qty →
Price on WTB; Qty ↔ Price on Selling).

## Compatibility

Buyer discovery uses new wire commands (`WQ`/`WR` for "who wants this item", `W`/`WC` for the
buyer scan, `WL`/`WK` for one buyer's list). Older clients don't answer them, so they're invisible
to buyer searches but everything else keeps working. The shared online/offline toggle gates both
selling and buying. Sellers and buyers on 0.12.x and 0.13.0 interoperate without changes.
