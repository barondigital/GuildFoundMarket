# Guild Found Market 0.15.0

Your listings can now track your bags. Turn on "Bag sync" for a listing and its quantity follows
how many you carry; sell out and it parks itself instead of vanishing. You can also park any
listing by hand, keeping it on your tab while hiding it from buyers.

## Added

**Park a listing instead of removing it.**
Set a listing's quantity to 0 (edit it, or let Bag sync empty it) to *park* it.

- A parked listing stays on your My Items tab but is hidden from everyone else: it stops answering
  searches, seller browses and shop links, and no longer counts towards a shop announce.
- Set a quantity above 0 to bring it back live.
- Parked rows show an orange `0`, with a footer note telling you how many are parked.
- It is the soft alternative to removing a listing when you are temporarily out of stock but want
  to keep its price and variant around.

**Optional per-listing "Bag sync".**
Each listing has a Bag-sync toggle: a column on the My Items list, and a checkbox in the compose
panel when you add or edit one.

- When on, GFM keeps that listing's quantity equal to how many you carry: it falls as you sell or
  use them and rises as you restock. At 0 it parks, never deleted.
- When off it is a manual claim, the right choice for stock kept on a bank alt, so one character
  can mix tracked and manual listings.
- A new Option, "New listings follow my bags", sets the default for newly created listings (off by
  default); changing it updates the compose checkbox live.
- Random-enchant variants are counted separately, the same way listings are keyed.
- It reads only on settled bag updates and skips while the cursor holds an item or just after a
  loading screen, so a transient 0 (zoning, a stack split) can never wipe a listing.

## Not changing

No protocol change: parked listings are simply filtered out before any reply leaves your client,
and Bag sync is a local, per-character setting. Older clients see exactly what they saw before.
