# Guild Found Market 0.8.2

A couple of small quality-of-life fixes. Grab it from CurseForge and run a full game restart (not just `/reload`), since the addon version changed.

## Changed

**Minimap right-click toggles your listings online/offline.**
Right-click the minimap icon to go online or offline, the same as the Online/Offline button on the My Items tab, without opening the window. The icon greys out while you are offline, so you can see your status at a glance, and the tooltip lists both actions (left-click to open the window, right-click to toggle).

**The marketplace channel no longer grabs channel /1.**
On login the addon used to join its hidden marketplace channel before your default chat channels (General, Trade, ...), so it could take channel /1 and shift all your channel numbers. It now waits for the default channels to settle first and joins after them, on a higher slot, out of the way. (The exact number depends on your setup and addons like GreenWall; the point is it no longer steals the low slots from your chat.)

## Note

If you are coming straight from 0.6.0 or 0.7.0, 0.8.x is the big "browse the market" release (a category browse, an installed-version display, and a cross-version fix). See the 0.8.1 notes for the full story. Players on 0.7.0 should update, since its message format is not compatible with 0.6.0 or 0.8.x.
