# Guild Found Market 0.10.0

The Announce button can now post to more than guild chat, with an optional shared trade channel, plus a per-player filter to hide shop-link spam. No wire-protocol or saved-listing changes, so it stays compatible with 0.9.x. Grab it from CurseForge and run a full game restart.

## Added

**Announce to a destination of your choice.**
The Announce button now has a destination picker next to it: guild, party, whisper, or your guild's trade channel. The whisper option has a name field that autocompletes guild members. As before, nothing is sent automatically: the line is composed into your chat box and you press Enter yourself. Your last destination is remembered per character.

**Trade channel for announces (guild setup).**
Officers can name a shared trade channel in the guild Information text:

    GFMtc:FreshTrade        (no password)
    GFMtc:FreshTrade:secret (with a password)

Members then get that channel as an announce destination, and can one-click join it straight from the picker (using the password if set). With no `GFMtc` line the option is shown greyed with a tooltip.

**Hide shop-link spam.**
New Options toggles let you hide incoming shop-link lines per chat surface: guild, party, whispers, and channels. This is local to you and changes nothing for anyone else; it only affects what you see.

## Changed

**Help tab reorganised.**
The Help tab is split into two sections at the top, Usage (how to buy, sell, and announce) and Guild Setup (the `GFMc` marketplace line, the new `GFMtc` trade channel, and sister-guild setup).
