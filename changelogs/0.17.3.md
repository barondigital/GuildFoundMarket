# Guild Found Market 0.17.3

Buyers just whisper "cod" and it lands on your list; Done tells the buyer, and the version is clickable.

## Added

**Capture a COD straight from a whisper.**
Almost nobody used the Alt-click affordance; buyers just whisper. So a COD order is now captured
from a plain whisper. When someone whispers you `cod`, `cod me 3`, `cod 20`, `[Item] cod 5`, or the
like about one of your listings, it's added to your COD list automatically at your listed price, with
the same on-screen flash and confirmation whisper as before. The parsing is forgiving: the word
"cod" and the quantity can sit anywhere in the message, with words between them, and coin amounts
(`80s`, `1g`) are never mistaken for a quantity. `all` / `cod me all` requests your whole listed
quantity. The item is read from a link in the whisper, or from the last item linked in your
conversation with that buyer, so the classic haggle ("[Citrine]@1g" ... "80s?" ... "sure" ...
"cod 5 then") still resolves to the right item. Only items you currently list are captured, and a
negotiated price is a one-click edit on the COD row. Turn the whole thing off with "Capture COD from
whispers" in Options (on by default).

**Done whispers the buyer, and X drops an order.**
Marking a COD order Done now whispers the buyer that it's on its way, configurable as the "COD mailed
whisper" (with the same tokens as the confirmation whisper, and a Reset to default). Each COD row
also has an X now, to remove an order without mailing it and without a whisper, distinct from Done.

**Click the version to read the changelog.**
The version text in the top right is clickable: it opens the changelog overlay, showing the newer
version's notes if you're behind and have received them from a peer, or your own build's notes to
re-read.
