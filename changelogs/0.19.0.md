# Guild Found Market 0.19.0

The bag scan now finds buyers per item, with a buyers window and one-click COD mail, plus big COD
mailing fixes and clearer player locations.

## Added

**The bag scan finds buyers too.**
Scan bags now asks the confederation who is buying as well as who is selling: alongside each
seller's price catalog it fetches every buyer's want list and matches both against your bags. On
the wire that is one extra broadcast plus one directed fetch per answering buyer, the same shape
and load as the price side. A new Buyers column (coin icon) on each scanned row lights up as soon
as the scan finds buyers for that item; the hover tells you how many.

**Buyers window.**
Clicking the coin opens a side window between the main window and the Debug/Network sidebar (an
open sidebar shifts right to make room) listing every buyer for that item: the amount they want
and the price they pay, best payer on top. "Offers" rows carry no price; those buyers want to
negotiate. Right-click a name to whisper. Hovering the item title shows the exact variant. The
window closes automatically when you leave the Scan tab.

**Send a COD straight from the buyers window.**
Each buyer row has a Send button that pre-fills the open mailbox's Send Mail exactly like the COD
tab's send-assist: recipient, subject, the amount they want (clamped to the unbound copies your
bags hold right now) and COD set to their price for that amount. You still press the real Send
button yourself. Buyers without a price have no COD amount to collect, so their button points you
to a whisper instead.

**Location columns show the zone.**
Player locations now travel as "SubZone, Zone": list columns show just the zone ("Orgrimmar"
instead of "The Drag") and hovering the player reveals the full location under their name and
guild. Older clients simply display the fuller string, which is an upgrade there too.

## Fixed

**The mailed-COD whisper no longer creates a phantom order.**
"Mailed your COD: X x2" contains the word cod plus a quantity, so on a buyer who also lists that
item it could re-capture as a brand-new COD order. All transactional COD whispers now carry a
capture-guard marker that even older clients honour, and the marker survives however long your
custom whisper templates are: the text is clamped into WoW's 255-character chat limit with the
marker included.

**The COD send-assist can split stacks and combine them.**
It used to require the whole order in one bag stack, and splitting silently attached the entire
source stack (a 21-item order shipped 2x20). Orders are now gathered across stacks: an exact-size
stack goes whole, fractured stacks are consumed before a full one is broken, and at most one stack
is split for the remainder. The split is parked in a free bag slot first and attached once the
server settles it, so the mail always carries exactly the ordered amount. Refusals now come before
the mail frame is touched, with honest messages: how many you actually have, when an order cannot
fit one mail's 12 attachment slots, or when no bag slot is free to park the split in.
