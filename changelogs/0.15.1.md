# Guild Found Market 0.15.1

Bag sync grew up: a synced listing now follows your whole stock on this character, not just your
bags. Keep mats in the bank or a stack sitting in the mailbox and the listing still reflects them,
without parking itself the moment you walk away from the banker.

## Changed

**Bag sync counts bags + bank + mailbox.**
A listing with Bag sync on now tracks the total of:

- your **bags**, read live;
- your **bank**, from a snapshot taken whenever you open it;
- your **mailbox**, from a snapshot taken whenever you open it.

The bank and mail can only be read while their window is open, so GFM keeps a per-character
snapshot of each and never overwrites it while that source is closed. That means walking away from
the bank can't read it as empty and park your listing, the bug-shaped behaviour that kept Bag sync
to bags-only before.

- **Multi-item mail is handled.** A single mail can carry several item attachments (one "package"
  icon, many slots); every attachment is counted, with stacks summed.
- **Variants stay separate.** Random-enchant variants are counted per exact item, the same way
  listings are keyed.
- **Honest about freshness.** The Bag-sync tooltip shows when the bank and mail snapshots were last
  seen, and when unread mail is waiting (the minimap envelope) it nudges you to open your mailbox,
  since GFM can't read mail it hasn't opened.
- Stock on **another character** (a bank alt) still isn't counted, so leave Bag sync off for those
  listings. The Options default is renamed to "New listings use Bag sync".

## Not changing

No protocol change: the stock cache is entirely local and per character, and parked listings are
still filtered out before any reply leaves your client. Older clients see exactly what they saw
before.
