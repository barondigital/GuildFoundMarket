# Guild Found Market 0.16.1

Every player's guild now shows on hover, wherever their name appears.

## Added

**A player's guild shows on hover.**
Hover a name anywhere it appears, the Buy results, the Sellers and Buyers lists, "who wants this",
the Browse results, and the header of an opened seller or buyer, and the tooltip's title reads
`Name <Guild>`. It stays a hover so it never costs list space and needs no setting to turn on or
off. A guild fills in once that player has answered one of your searches or scans this session, and
only for players on this version or newer; until then you just see the name.

## Changed

**The Browse hover shows location too.**
The Browse seller column is too narrow for much, so its hover now also lists the seller's location
under the name, matching the other lists. If a seller on an older client sends no location, GFM
falls back to one it already learned for them in a Sellers scan.

## Protocol

Backward compatible (append-only). The replies whose sender we display, search (`R`),
category browse (`QR`), the seller and buyer summaries (`C` / `WC`), and the buyer-find reply
(`WR`), now carry the responder's guild as a trailing field, and the category-browse reply (`QR`)
also carries the seller's location, which it never did before. Older clients read the existing
fields unchanged and ignore the new ones, so 0.16.0 and earlier keep interoperating; they simply
send no guild (and no Browse location) of their own.
