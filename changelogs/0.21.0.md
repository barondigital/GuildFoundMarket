# Guild Found Market 0.21.0

Your WTB list can now scan the market for sellers with one click, item whispers open with
[WTB] or [WTS] so the receiver instantly knows which side of the trade you are on, and Bag
sync stops counting copies that went soulbound.

## Added

**Scan sellers on My Items > WTB.**
One button next to your WTB list sweeps the whole market for everything on it: GFM asks who is
selling, then walks every answering seller's price list, the same way the bag scan does. The
results open in a sellers window right of the main one: each wanted item as a header, under it
everyone who sells it with their stock and price, cheapest first and "bids" offers last. You
see live progress, can stop at any time, and right-click a seller to whisper them with the item
pre-filled. The window belongs to the WTB view: switch away and it closes, stopping a scan
still running. Every price it saw is remembered for item tooltips.

**Item whispers open with [WTB] or [WTS].**
Right-click whispers from any list now state your intent up front: whispering a seller starts
with [WTB] (you want to buy from them), whispering a buyer starts with [WTS] (you want to sell
to them), followed by the item link and the price under discussion. The buyers and sellers side
windows, which used to open an empty whisper, now pre-fill the item and price too.

## Fixed

**Bag sync counts only what you can actually trade.**
A "follow my bags" listing kept counting a copy that became soulbound (for example by equipping
a BoE), so its quantity stayed too high. Bag and bank scans now skip bound copies: a copy that
turns soulbound drops out of your listed quantity on the next bag update, exactly as if it left
your bags. A bank snapshot taken before this update corrects itself the next time you open the
bank.
